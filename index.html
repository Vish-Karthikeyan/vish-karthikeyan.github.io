About Me:
Hello, I am Viswajeeth (Vish) Karthikeyan. My name literally means “conqueror of the universe” in Sanskrit (विश्वजित्), but so far the only thing I have conquered (with significant collateral damage) is a solid foundation in computer science, economics and political science at one of the world’s leading universities.
I’m officially a CS major on the theory track (yes, we exist) with a minor in Public Policy, but my interests broadly include statistical learning, natural language processing, complexity theory, public decision-making, constructive dialogue, and education. You can look at my current research work [here](/projects/), and my teaching [here](/teaching/).
I grew up in Erode, India, in the southernmost state of Tamil Nadu surrounded by textile looms, vibrant paddy fields and a community shaped by diligence and ambition. At Stanford, I am a part of the Stanford Political Union’s leadership team—THE club for fostering non-partisan, constructive dialogue on campus that goes as far as 1953. I've helped organize discussions, speaker events and debates featuring figures like former Secretary of Defense Jim Mattis, former Health Secretary Xavier Becerra, former Justice Stephen Breyer, and Andrew Yang, creating space for open, thoughtful discussions for the entire Stanford community.
You might also find me working at the Bridge Peer Counseling Center, Stanford's student-run peer counseling service open 24/7 for calls and walk-ins! Or maybe you ended up on this page after you saw my work in the Stanford Political Review.
Lately, I've been diving into Spanish—often between shifts at Arrillaga Family Dining Commons (no, I don't pick the menu, sorry!). ¡Hablamos pronto!
