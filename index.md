Hello, I am Viswajeeth (Vish) Karthikeyan (he/him). I’m a Computer Science major at Stanford University on the Artificial Intelligence track with a minor in Public Policy. My interests broadly include statistical learning, natural language processing, public decision-making, constitutional law and technology policy, constructive dialogue, and education. You can look at my current research work [here](/research/), and my teaching [here](/discourse/#teaching).

I grew up in Erode, India, in the southernmost state of Tamil Nadu, surrounded by textile looms, vibrant paddy fields and a community shaped by diligence and ambition. As a recipient of the Dhirubhai Ambani Scholarship, I moved across the world to Stanford to ponder recklessly and build deliberately.

Outside of academics and research, I am a part of the Stanford Political Union’s leadership team—THE club for fostering non-partisan, constructive dialogue on campus, dating back to 1953. You might also find me working at the Bridge Peer Counseling Center, Stanford’s student-run peer counseling service, open 24/7 for calls and walk-ins! Or maybe you ended up on this page after you saw my work in the *Stanford Political Review*.

I love reading (check out my [recommendations](/reading/)!), getting into a respectful argument with you, and driving around to random places in a Zipcar! If you would like to chat about any of my work, feel free to [reach out](mailto:vkarthikeyan@stanford.edu)!
